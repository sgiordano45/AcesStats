How the board works

The board decides

Rules, rosters, discipline, major policies and other significant league decisions go to a board vote.

The Commissioner carries it out

The Commissioner runs the league day to day and puts the board's decisions into practice.

The town has final say

The Recreation Department keeps whatever final authority it needs, as set out in the league's governing document.

Decisions are published

Board decisions are posted here so every team knows what was decided and when.
